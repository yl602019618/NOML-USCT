About NOML-USCT

Rigorous mathematics
for a new imaging frontier.

Ultrasound computed tomography is a promising radiation-free and cost-effective modality for quantitative imaging of biological tissue, but its reconstruction is governed by nonlinear and severely ill-posed inverse problems. NOML-USCT develops a mathematical and computational foundation for this challenge by bringing together nonsmooth optimization, variational regularization, PDE-constrained modeling, and machine learning.

Our goal is to build reconstruction methods that are not only efficient, but also stable, interpretable, and supported by rigorous analysis. Led by Michael Hintermüller, Guozhi Dong, and Zuoqiang Shi, this NSFC–DFG collaboration connects complementary expertise in optimization, inverse problems, numerical analysis, and scientific machine learning across Germany and China.
